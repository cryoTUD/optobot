# 02 Climate chamber

The climate chamber module consists of 4 subassemblies, which are the following:
- 02 Climate chamber
- 02.1 Humidifier chamber
- 02.2 Reservoir
- 02.3 Valve assembly

Together, these subassemblies result in a climate chamber that can be humidified, where the humidity can be maintained. This results in that within the plunging process, the samples can be deposited onto the Grid in an already fully humidified environment. The Climate chamber assembly itself (02) is the enclosed chamber in which the tweezers with the grid are placed during sample deposition and blotting. This climate chamber acts as the humidified working environment during all the steps prior to the actual plunge. The humidifier chamber (02.1) itself is where the mist is generated that is then through a fan steered into the climate chamber. The reservoir (02.2) acts as a water reservoir for the humidifier chamber, providing it with water. The valve assembly (02.3) controls whether the reservoir is connected to the humidifier chamber or to the filling syringe. More on that below.

Within this ReadMe the following will be discussed for these assemblies:
  - Overview picture of assembly
  - Exploded view with part list
  - Assembly animation (with additional assembly notes)
In addition to these points, the working principles of the Assembly will be explained.

There are a couple of parts within the climate chamber assembly that serve a specific function. The slider on the right wall serves as the sample inlet opening that can be opened and closed to contain the humidity. The servo along with the shutter plate together also ensure that the climate chamber is closed at the times the tweezers and grid are hanging in the chamber. By closing the bottom hole, the humidity is better contained. During the actual plunge, the servo then rotates the shutter plate away so that the tweezers can be moved through the bottom hole in the base plate. The humidity tubes are connected to the tube inlets that are mounted on the back plate. These humidity tubes are directly connected to the humidifier chamber and transfer the mist from the humidifier chamber into the climate chamber.

The humidifier tube that transports the mist towards the climate chamber is connected to the sides of the humidifier chamber at the tube inlets. - The round horizontal chamber divider is glued onto the vertical chamber divider.

  ## 02.3 Valve assembly
Below, pictures of the assembly and the exploded view with the parts list are shown.

<img src = "images/02.3_Valve_assembly/Valve Assembly Isometric.png" width = "1000">

<img width="701" height="720" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/ec9ade67-b050-4ddb-8bbf-aa72c04ac4f9" />

The valve assembly is used to control whether the reservoir is connected to the humidifier chamber, or whether the 60 ml filling syringe is connected to the humidifier chamber or reservoir. To the top pneumatic fitting of the valve assembly, the reservoir is connected. To the bottom, the humidifier is connected. To the right, the 60 mL filling syringe is connected, which is used for refilling the reservoir and/or the humidifier chamber.

The lever can then be used to control which of these three are connected to one another. The line marking on the lever near the rotation point can be used as an indicator for which parts are connected. In the provided picture of the assembly, the line marking on the lever currently connects the top and bottom pneumatic fittings. In practice, those fittings are actually connected, meaning that the reservoir is now connected to the humidifier chamber. When one rotates the lever 90 degrees clockwise, the markings on the lever indicate that the top fitting and the right fittings are connected. And again, this is the case in practice. In this orientation of the lever, the valve connects the filling syringe to the reservoir. Now, in this orientation, the reservoir can be refilled.

In order to refill, first fill up the syringe. Then connect to the pneumatic hose that is connected to the right pneumatic fitting of the valve assembly. Finally, squeeze the syringe so that all the water is pushed through the valve assembly into the reservoir.
