# 02 Climate chamber

The climate chamber module consists of 4 subassemblies, which are the following:
- 02 Climate chamber
- 02.1 Humidifier chamber
- 02.2 Reservoir
- 02.3 Valve assembly

Together, these subassemblies result in a climate chamber that can be humidified, where the humidity can be maintained. This results in that within the plunging process, the samples can be deposited onto the Grid in an already fully humidified environment. The Climate chamber assembly itself (02) is the enclosed chamber in which the tweezers with the grid are placed during sample deposition and blotting. This climate chamber acts as the humidified working environment during all the steps prior to the actual plunge. The humidifier chamber (02.1) itself is where the mist is generated that is then through a fan steered into the climate chamber. The reservoir (02.2) acts as a water reservoir for the humidifier chamber, providing it with water. The valve assembly (02.3) controls whether the reservoir is connected to the humidifier chamber or to the filling syringe. More on that below.

Within this ReadMe the following will be discussed for these assemblies:
  - Overview picture of assembly
  - Exploded view with part list
  - Assembly animation (with additional assembly notes)
In addition to these points, the working principles of the Assembly will be explained.

There are a couple of parts within the climate chamber assembly that serve a specific function. The slider on the right wall serves as the sample inlet opening that can be opened and closed to contain the humidity. The servo along with the shutter plate together also ensure that the climate chamber is closed at the times the tweezers and grid are hanging in the chamber. By closing the bottom hole, the humidity is better contained. During the actual plunge, the servo then rotates the shutter plate away so that the tweezers can be moved through the bottom hole in the base plate. The humidity tubes are connected to the tube inlets that are mounted on the back plate. These humidity tubes are directly connected to the humidifier chamber and transfer the mist from the humidifier chamber into the climate chamber.

For the climate chamber, an assembly animation is also created that, as a starting point, has an exploded view.

The climate chamber door is actively closing due to the door magnets that are placed into the base plate behind the door lid. In the animation above, their location can be observed more clearly.  

Additional assembly notes:
- The door hinges are bolted onto the base plate using nuts that are placed on the inside of the pillars
- The door magnets are glued into the base plate pillars
- The door lid is glued onto the base plate pillars
- Thread is first tapped into the door for the mounting of the steel bolts. The steel bolts serve as the magnetic counterpart for the magnets. Together, they create the self-closing feature of the door.
- Quite often, when bolts or screws are directly mounted onto 3d printed structures, the holes on the 3d printed structures are tapped first with the corresponding thread.

Below is a picture of the assembly. Right after, the exploded view with the corresponding part list is shown.

The humidifier tube that transports the mist towards the climate chamber is connected to the sides of the humidifier chamber at the tube inlets. At the front of the humidifier, two pneumatic fittings are placed. The left fitting is directly connected through pneumatic tubing to the left fitting of the reservoir. The right fitting is connected to the bottom side of the lever assembly.

### Assembly instructions: 02.1 Humidifier chamber assembly
For the humidifier chamber, an assembly animation is also created that, as a starting point, has an exploded view.

https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/509ea023-911e-4862-a4f4-a3f68c2f4d24

Additional assembly notes:
- Before mounting the pneumatic fitting, first tap the humidifier holes to recreate the thread. Then the pneumatic fittings can be screwed on.
- The humidifier chamber should be hanging at a lower height than the reservoir, so that water will flow naturally from the reservoir into the humidifier chamber.
- The humidifier is directly bolted onto the support structure. For that, use a nut from the company item that fits in the profile grooves of the support structure.
- The round horizontal chamber divider is glued onto the vertical chamber divider.
