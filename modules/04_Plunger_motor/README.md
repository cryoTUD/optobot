# 04 Plunger motor

The Plunger motor consists of the following two assemblies:
- Lift assembly
- Plunger motor assembly

The lift assembly is responsible for raising the ethane cup towards the climate chamber. The Plunger motor assembly controls the vertical position of the Tweezer and, with that, the grid. Within this ReadMe both assemblies will be shown and discussed.

**An important general note for the entire module:**
We ordered all the parts for the Plunger motor module from Groneman.nl. We already did that in September 2020. At the time, we were in direct contact with Groneman, and together with them, we determined all the parts that were required for setting up the Plunger motor module. Since the parts used stem from 2020, some parts might no longer be available. Either new versions of these parts have appeared, or the parts need to be replaced with other similar types. We would therefore like to make the recommendation that, when trying to order and set up the plunger module, you reach out to a supplier (LinMot or Groneman, for example). Together with these suppliers, you can work out the exact parts you need. This ensures that all the parts within the Plunger module are suited to one another.

The plunger motor itself is a linear actuator that consists of a stator and a slider. The circlip at the end of the slider ensures that the slider can't fall through the stator. The stator is connected through the flange to the support structure

In the exploded view, the actual coupling used to couple the servo motor axle and the lift unit axle is not shown. That coupling sits within the coupling flange. The lift unit is a belt-driven unit that, by rotating the belt, can move the carriage up and down. The cup holder is directly connected to this carriage via the vertical connector. The lift unit itself is directly clamped onto the support structure through the clamping fixtures.

### Assembly instructions: Lift unit assembly
For the Lift unit assembly, a short assembly animation video is shown below.

https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/6e3db775-5a66-45ad-91db-678d1cf48ee9

Additional assembly notes:
- The lift clamping fixtures are directly bolted onto the support structure. For this, use a nut from the company item that fits in the profile grooves of the support structure.
- Tap the holes in the cup holder so they match the thread of the countersunk screw. The countersunk screw used to connect the cup holder and the vertical connector can then be inserted from the vertical connector side. When screwed into the thread of the cup holder holes, the cup holder and vertical connector are pulled together.
